# 🌿 FloraFocus - Botanical Pomodoro Focus Sanctuary

FloraFocus is a botanical focus timer and productivity sanctuary. Stay focused to nurture 25 procedural plant species, gather golden honey nectar with Barnabee the bee companion, and curate your personal garden sanctuary.

## ✨ Features
- ⏱️ **Adaptive Focus Timer**: Customizable sessions (15m, 25m, 45m, 60m) with ambient soundscapes.
- 🌸 **25 Procedural Plant Species**: Real-time 5-stage mathematical SVG growth engine.
- 🐝 **Barnabee Companion**: Interactive hovering companion that gathers honey during focus sessions.
- 🏺 **Seed Apothecary**: Unlock rare, exotic, and mythic botanical species.
- 🌿 **Sanctuary Garden**: Catalog your blossomed plants and track your focus milestones.
- 🎧 **Web Audio Synthesizer**: Procedural pink noise rain soundscapes and harmonic completion chimes.
- 📱 **PWA & Vercel Ready**: 1-click deploy to Vercel and 1-tap installation on Android/iOS.

## 🚀 1-Click Deployment (Vercel)
1. Import this repository into [Vercel](https://vercel.com/new).
2. Framework Preset: Other
3. Click **Deploy**!
