# 🌱 FloraFocus — Grow Your Focus Garden

> An engaging, gamified focus timer application where every focus session plants a seed that grows step-by-step as you focus. The duration selected determines the species and rarity of the plant you cultivate, building a persistent personal garden sanctuary over time.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. ⏱️ Dynamic Plant Evolution & Time Slots
- **15 min**: *Little Jade Succulent* (Tier 1) — Quick sprints & short tasks
- **30 min**: *Radiant Sunflower* (Tier 2) — Standard Pomodoro sprint
- **45 min**: *Zen Juniper Bonsai* (Tier 3) — Deep study & reading
- **60 min**: *Cherry Blossom Tree* (Tier 4) — Intense deep work hour
- **120 min**: *Ancient World Redwood* (Tier 5) — Master marathon flow session

### 2. 🌱 4-Stage Live Growth Visualizer
- **Stage 1 (0–24%)**: Seed planted in dark loam with gentle sunbeam halo.
- **Stage 2 (25–49%)**: Sprouting fresh stem and baby leaflets.
- **Stage 3 (50–74%)**: Branching sapling and budding flower heads.
- **Stage 4 (75–99%)**: Mature canopy swelling with color.
- **Stage 5 (100% Bloom)**: Fully bloomed botanical masterwork with aura glow & celebration chime!
- **🥀 Wither Mechanic**: If you prematurely give up or abandon the session, your plant withers into a dried twig.

### 3. 🏡 Garden Sanctuary (Collection Grid)
- A persistent virtual sanctuary showcasing every tree, flower, and bonsai you have cultivated.
- Click on any plant to inspect its completion timestamp, duration, tag, and write session achievement notes.
- Filter by tags (*Deep Work, Study, Reading, Writing, Creative, Meditation*) or status (*Bloomed vs. Withered*).

### 4. 🎧 Organic Ambient Soundscapes (Web Audio API)
- Pure synthesized natural soundscapes with independent volume controls:
  - 🌧️ *Gentle Rain on Leaves*
  - 🌲 *Forest Birds & Mountain Breeze*
  - 🪵 *Cozy Hearth Campfire*
  - 💧 *Mountain Brook & River Stream*
  - 🍃 *Soft Wind & Zen Chimes*
  - 🔔 *Triumphant Harmonic Bloom Chime on completion*

### 5. 📊 Insights & Habit Tracker
- Total focus time and session counter.
- Active daily streak 🔥 and best streak tracking.
- Species codex unlock progress (0 / 5).
- Focus quality / bloom rate percentage.
- 7-day weekly focus bar chart.
- Complete JSON export & import backup.

### 6. ⚡ Testing & Demonstration Tools
- **10x / 30x Speed Mode**: Instantly fast-forward growth animations to test all stages in seconds.
- **Zen Fullscreen Mode**: Distraction-free ambient backdrop with floating pollen and drifting sakura petals.

## 🚀 How to Run

### Option A: Open directly in your browser
Simply double-click `index.html` or open it with Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, or Safari:
```text
C:\Users\frame\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\flora-focus-timer\index.html
```

### Option B: Run via Node.js Server
Open a terminal in this directory and run:
```powershell
& 'C:\Users\frame\AppData\Roaming\Antigravity\bin\agy-node.cmd' server.js
```
Then visit:
```text
http://localhost:3000
```
